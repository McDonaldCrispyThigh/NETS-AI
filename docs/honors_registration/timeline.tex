April 2026
&
Honors Program registration packet submitted to the Arts and Sciences Honors Program (deadline April 22, 2026).
Board of Pharmacy validation pipeline completed; 399 AI-collected records matched against 459 Board licensure records.
Multi-stage validity audit completed; adjusted F1 score 79.9\% documented with institutional contamination analysis.
Figures B1 through B5 generated and saved to repository; figure manifest updated.
\\[0.8em]

Late April -- Early May 2026
&
NETS commercial database received from research team (expected April 27, 2026).
NETS vs.\ Board of Pharmacy validation completed using identical fuzzy-matching protocol as Board validation.
Figure B4 finalized with NETS comparison data replacing pending placeholder bars.
\\[0.8em]

May -- June 2026
&
Literature review chapter finalized; coverage to include pharmacy desert research, commercial database validation literature, and GeoAI methodological work.
Introduction chapter drafted.
Methodology chapter drafted; pipeline architecture, matching protocol, and audit procedure documented in full.
Summer research assistant work begins under supervision of Prof.\ Ellen Considine, CU Boulder.
\\[0.8em]

July 2026
&
IMGS 2026 conference presentation in Toronto (GeoTranslator abstract, acceptance pending); preliminary findings presented to the international GIS community.
Results chapter drafted; Board validation, NETS comparison, income quartile spatial analysis, and North Minneapolis case study documented.
Committee check-in with Prof.\ Finlay, Prof.\ Travis, and Prof.\ Becker to review progress and confirm chapter scope.
\\[0.8em]

August 2026
&
Discussion chapter drafted; interpretation of income gradient non-finding, FQHC policy implications, and methodological contributions developed.
Full draft circulated to thesis committee (Finlay, Travis, Becker) for comments.
\\[0.8em]

September 2026
&
Committee feedback incorporated into revised draft.
Second full draft submitted to thesis advisor (Prof.\ Finlay) for detailed review.
\\[0.8em]

October 2026
&
Defense copy finalized following advisor review.
Oral defense scheduled; target date late October or early November 2026.
Defense copy distributed to committee at least two weeks prior to defense date.
\\[0.8em]

November 2026

Oral defense conducted before thesis committee.
Final revisions completed following defense.
Final copy submitted to CU Scholar institutional repository.

Graduation with honors, University of Colorado Boulder.
